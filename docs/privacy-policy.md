# Gishath Fetch — Privacy Policy

**Canonical URL:** [https://gishathfetch.com/?privacy=1](https://gishathfetch.com/?privacy=1)

**Last updated:** 29 August 2026

This policy describes how [gishathfetch.com](https://gishathfetch.com/) handles information when
you use the website. The Gishath Fetch Telegram bot
([@GishathFetchBot](https://t.me/GishathFetchBot)) has a
[separate privacy policy](https://gishathfetch.com/telegram-bot-privacy.html).

## Access Logs

This website collects personal data through its server access logs. When you access this
website, your internet address is automatically collected and placed in our access logs. We
record the URLs of the pages you visit, the times and dates of such visits.

This information may include Internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type and version,
internet service provider (ISP), referring/exit pages, operating system, date/time stamp,
and/or clickstream data, number of visits, websites from which you accessed our site
(Referrer), and websites that are accessed by your system via our website.

The processing of this data is necessary for the provision and the security of this website.

## Search Session Cookie

Before you run a search, the site may request a short-lived HttpOnly session cookie
(`gf_api_session`) from our search API at `api.gishathfetch.com`. The cookie helps protect
the search API from abuse. It is not used to identify you personally or to track you across
other websites.

## Google Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics. Google Analytics employs cookies that are stored on your
computer to facilitate an analysis of your use of the website. The information generated
by these cookies, such as time, place and frequency of your visits to our site, including
your IP address, is transmitted to Google.

When you search for a card, we also send the **search term** (the card name or text you
entered) to Google Analytics as an event parameter. We use this in aggregate form to
understand usage and to power features such as **Trending**. We do not use it to build a
profile of you as an identifiable person.

Google Analytics offers a deactivation add-on for most current browsers that provides you
with more control over what data Google can collect on websites you access. You can find
additional information about the add-on
[here](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## Google AdSense

This website uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners may use
cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other
websites, and to measure ad performance. Ad-related data is processed by Google under
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

You can manage ad personalisation through [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).

## Telegram Bot

Gishath Fetch is also available as a Telegram bot
([@GishathFetchBot](https://t.me/GishathFetchBot)). The bot has a
[separate privacy policy](https://gishathfetch.com/telegram-bot-privacy.html) that explains
